Spirits of San Francisco: A Photo Companion

An ongoing project photographing the places, corners, and hidden histories from Gary Kamiya's and Paul Madonna's Spirits of San Francisco: Voyages Through the Unknown City — matching my own photos to the book's stories and drawings as I make my way through it, chapter by chapter. Click a marker on the map to see photos for that place, or scroll down for the full list.

📖 The book: Spirits of San Francisco: Voyages Through the Unknown City by Gary Kamiya and Paul Madonna is the companion to this page — every place here comes from something Kamiya wrote about and Madonna illustrated.
